# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mobile users who want to ask questions, access specialist AI assistance, and manage their account without navigating a dense desktop-style interface.

## Product Purpose

ChatCafe is a mobile AI assistant. Its primary navigation must make switching between conversation, specialist assistance, and account management immediate and understandable.

## Brand Personality

Light, trustworthy, and approachable. The product should feel calm and responsive rather than ornamental or game-like.

## Anti-references

Detached floating controls, excessive decorative motion, and navigation that competes visually with the content it controls.

## Design Principles

- Keep primary actions easy to scan and easy to reach on a phone.
- Use emphasis to clarify current location, not to decorate the interface.
- Preserve familiar navigation affordances and stable tap targets.
- Keep motion short, purposeful, and optional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, readable contrast, and reduced-motion behavior are default requirements.
